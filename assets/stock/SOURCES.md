## Notes

- **Kebab photograph:** cropped to 4:5 (box `120, 170, 1560, 1970` from the 1600 × 2000 original) to remove third-party paper printing and handwriting visible at the edges. The crop is still a placeholder, not a photograph of ALMAS food.
- **Pizza sizes:** the pizza photographs show a Neapolitan-style pizza. The menu's pizzas are not confirmed to be Neapolitan; these are atmosphere images, not product-accurate images.
- **Menu gallery:** the kebab, pizza and baklava images are category-level atmosphere images marked "Symbolbild" / "Placeholder" on the menu. They are not photographs of ALMAS dishes and must be replaced before launch.
- **Unused candidate:** `tea-1600.jpg` / `tea-900.jpg` are retained from the asset exploration but are not referenced by any prototype page. Their source was not recorded. They should be removed or re-sourced before production.
- **Logo files:** `prototypes/assets/logo-mark.svg` and `prototypes/assets/favicon.svg` are original recreations for this project based on the client's A + diamond idea. They are not stock assets.
- **No real ALMAS photography** is included in the prototype. Interior video frames and menu photographs remain in `assets/source/` as client reference material only.
- Before launch: replace every stock image, add a real photography credit/licence record, and confirm that the restaurant owns or has licensed all final images.
